I'm an undergraduate student at **Yonsei University**, majoring in Artificial
Intelligence, and a research intern at
[CIPLAB](https://www.ciplab.kr) with Prof. Seon Joo Kim.

My research interest lies in **Generative AI**, with an emphasis on its
theoretical foundations and on the *geometry of generative trajectories* — the
paths along which generative models transport probability mass. Beyond theory,
I am interested in applying generative models to **Computational Photography**,
particularly toward user-preference-driven image editing and the design of
next-generation camera pipelines.
